FAQ

Straight answers

Are these documents legal advice?

No. They are well-structured starting templates built from your answers. Have a qualified attorney review anything before you sign it or send it into a procurement process.

Will this make me compliant with the EU AI Act?

No tool can certify that. What this does is produce the documentation buyers and reviewers ask for, structured around the EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, and ISO/IEC 42001 - and show you where your gaps are before someone else finds them.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere during the pilot. The questionnaire, the risk scoring, and the document generator all run in your browser. Your answers are stored in your own browser storage so you can come back and edit, and clearing that storage deletes them.

What does it cost?

Free during the pilot while we work with early users. If we charge later, pilot users keep their documents and get advance notice.
